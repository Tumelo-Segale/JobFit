How does JobFit compute scores securely offline?

1. Direct Extractor

PDF.js and Mammoth extract raw text from file blobs in your browser. No servers queried.

2. Pattern Processor

Local heuristics scan formatting, filenames, date patterns, and section structure.

3. Stem-Matched Keywords

JD keywords are Porter-stemmed so "engineer" matches "engineering". All computed locally.

Methodology note: JobFit uses heuristic pattern analysis - not semantic AI or an actual ATS system. Scores are directional indicators of structural quality, not guarantees of interview success.
